About Root Causes

A student-led organization at Duke University School of Medicine connecting communities with emergency food assistance and local resources.

Our Mission

Root Causes works to address food insecurity and connect community members with essential resources in Durham, NC. We take an asset-based approach — building on the strengths and resources that already exist within communities rather than focusing on deficits.

What We Do

Through this platform, we coordinate volunteer operations, manage participant outreach, track deliveries, and maintain a community resource directory. Community members can discover local resources and submit new ones for staff review.

Community Resources

The Community Resource board is open to everyone. You can browse approved resources, view them on a map, submit new resources for review, and even contribute grocery price reports through our partnership with OpenShelf.
